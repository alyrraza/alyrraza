## Selected projects

**[Property-Tech Vision AI Platform](https://alyrraza.vercel.app/projects/property-tech-vision-ai)** - flagship, confidential
A production computer vision and VLM pipeline that turns property photos into structured, customer-ready outputs for a property-tech SaaS platform serving US customers. Production details are confidential, happy to walk through the architecture in interviews.
`FastAPI` `Computer Vision` `VLM` `MLOps`

**[RadGuard](https://github.com/alyrraza/RadGuard-Medical-AI)** · [live demo](https://radguard.vercel.app/)
Catches 4 classes of error in AI-generated chest X-ray reports. BioViL-T + CXR-BERT cross-attention over 74K MIMIC-CXR samples, macro F1 0.66 after root-causing a patient-level data leakage bug.
`PyTorch` `FastAPI` `Docker` `Kubernetes`

**[PIT-Scorecard](https://github.com/alyrraza/pit-scorecard)** · [live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/alyrraza/pit-scorecard)
Measured a +0.30 AUC leakage gap hiding in a standard credit scorecard, then shipped the honest version: a WOE/IV scorecard validated on a strict out-of-time split across 2.26M loans.
`Python` `LightGBM` `FastAPI` `DuckDB`

**[DocDesk](https://github.com/alyrraza/docdesk-healthcare-rag-evals)**
A medical RAG service that cites its sources, abstains when they do not cover a question, and refuses dose or diagnosis questions outright, validated by an in-house LLM evaluation harness with paired bootstrap intervals.
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `Docker`

**[E2E-VarNet MRI Reconstruction](https://github.com/alyrraza/e2e-varnet-mri-reconstruction)** · [live demo](https://e2e-varnet-mri-reconstruction.vercel.app/)
Reproduced E2E-VarNet for 4x-accelerated MRI reconstruction and added a checkpoint-ensemble uncertainty signal that flags out-of-distribution scans, backed by Evidently AI drift monitoring.
`PyTorch` `Gradio` `Evidently AI`
